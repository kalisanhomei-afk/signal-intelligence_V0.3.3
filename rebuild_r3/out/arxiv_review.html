必須保留的研究語義

研究設計：作者描述 5 個 evaluation suites：1 個 in-distribution 評估，及 4 個泛化軸（空間配置、物件實例、物件類別、子任務組合）。不能誤寫成 5＋4＝9 個 suite。

方法：摘要稱評估了 7 種基準方法；作者描述 2 個平台：單臂 MESA、雙臂 BiMESA。

研究結果：摘要所述最佳模型為 MESA 52%、BiMESA 49%。摘要沒有足夠資訊讓我們確認這兩個百分比的測量定義、分母、統計信賴程度、泛化效果或實際場域表現；不得稱為「準確率」或「成功率」。

宣稱限制：「第一個大規模雙臂泛化基準」是作者提出的優先性宣稱，目前未獨立比對文獻。

保存的作者摘要摘錄 E1–E6

E1｜作者摘要摘錄

Despite growing interest in foundation models for robotic manipulation, systematic evaluation of their capabilities remains elusive.

E2｜作者摘要摘錄

Existing benchmarks are either narrow and saturated, offering little signal on SOTA methods, broadly scoped, providing diversity at the cost of controlled experimentation, or require training on large, messy open-source datasets.

E3｜作者摘要摘錄

We introduce MESA, an evaluation framework designed for precise, reproducible measurement of language-conditioned policy generalization.

E4｜作者摘要摘錄

MESA provides five evaluation suites that isolate in-distribution performance and four distinct axes of generalization: spatial configuration, object instance, object category, and subtask composition, enabling researchers to diagnose where and why policies fail, not just whether they fail.

E5｜作者摘要摘錄

To populate these suites at scale, we develop MESA-Gen, a pipeline for task and demonstration generation built by improving MimicGen. We instantiate the framework on two platforms and release both: the single-arm MESA suite, and BiMESA, the first large-scale benchmark for bimanual generalization.

E6｜作者摘要摘錄

We evaluate seven baseline methods spanning diffusion policies, VLM-based approaches, and pretrained VLAs, finding that the strongest model achieves only 52% on MESA and 49% on BiMESA, providing meaningful signal while leaving ample room for progress.
