## 3. 反證與替代機制

- **Current evidence DISCONFIRMS the universal premise** that both all equities and all physical-gold ETF investment were simultaneously witnessing negative net subscriptions across the cited reporting periods: ICI equity combined +US$37.020bn (Nov 11 week) and WGC gold ETF +US$2.7bn (October). This does **not** disprove an individual day's sell-off, location-specific outflows, gold derivatives de-risking, or investor reallocations outside reported funds.
- Valuation effects are non-cash flows: gold ETF **AUM -6%** despite reported inflows and tonnage increase. ETF **net issuance**, MMF net asset changes, TIC financial-account transactions and BIS adjusted bank credit changes are **different statistical objects**. Sum or netting across them is **invalid**.
- **Potential other channels remain UNKNOWN**: margin/leveraged futures liquidation, currency hedging, bank deposit creation/repayment, direct bond buying, sovereign cash and repo; none is "the destination" merely because another asset price declined.
- Independent competing explanations: (a) price-led liquidation with buyer substitution, (b) simultaneous new credit and collateral deleveraging in distinct investor groups, (c) revaluation without matching redemptions, (d) fund wrapper migration from mutual funds to ETFs, (e) geographically heterogeneous demand.
